# Digital ontogeny migration target

Phase 2B.5 does **not** implement the Morphogenesis Lab and does not rename or
rewrite the Phase 1 `DigitalNeuron` runtime. Backward compatibility is required.
This document is the target for a later, evidence-driven migration.

The fundamental evolvable unit will be a pluripotent **DigitalCell**. A neuron is
one phenotype, not the only possible cell. A future Digital Meristem will preserve
pluripotent cells while transient organoids may form and dissolve.

Saphira-A and Saphira-B have permanent, separate developmental lineages. Each
sister develops private cognition, memory, and morphology. Neither sister may
overwrite, absorb, or evolve into the other lineage. Together, A+B and their shared
external G.O.D. Body form the higher-order twin superorganism called G.O.D.

The sisters may use the shared Body only through an authority-controlled `BodyBus`.
The shared Root Constitution remains outside evolution. Root authority, including
owner approval and credential custody, is never a cellular phenotype.

Future experiments may investigate morphogen fields, stigmergy, lateral inhibition,
tropisms, resource source-sink dynamics, canalization, immune waves, and
regeneration. These are hypotheses: developmental laws will be empirically tested
in M0 rather than hardcoded as truth.

## M0-1 laboratory protocol

M0-1 is a lab-only falsification tournament; it is not wired to `ModelBus`,
`BodyBus`, credentials, Root, or production cognition.  H0 is a matched fixed-graph
control; H1 is APGC + developmental genome + MUF; H2 is NCA-like local plasticity;
H3 mutates topology and retains changes only through measured proxies without MUF;
H4 coordinates through a persistent stigmergic trace; and H5 is
active-inference-like because local surprise, rather than full variational active
inference, drives adaptation. H1 additionally competes against no-MUF,
fixed-topology, no-differentiation, and no-prediction-coupling ablations.

MUF is generated only by `B*(t)-B(t)` through the common bounded deficit-source
mapping. Field update is bounded local diffusion plus local emitter contribution;
effective perception is `M_eff[i,k] = receptor[i,k] * M[i,k]`. Cells propose
actions, while a resource governor alone permits population/topology changes.
Verified synthetic outcome deltas create developmental experience and bounded
competence/epigenetic updates.

Generalization uses a deep snapshot with learning disabled. Temporal memory has a
delayed query without the answer; transformation stages are separated; rule shift
has no shift flag; communication corruption changes inter-cell messages; and
damage stays present throughout measured recovery. Isolated and independent
baselines are real `DigitalCell` simulations with communication absent. Every
architecture is charged under the same operation convention; overruns are
persisted as `DISQUALIFIED`, never omitted.

Predictions are falsifiable: H1's MUF claim fails if it does not beat H1-no-MUF on
preregistered distributed/damage evidence within uncertainty; analogous paired
ablations govern topology, differentiation, and prediction claims. H0--H5
comparisons alone never establish a component claim. A one-seed quick run is CI
evidence only; research mode supports `god-ontogeny --seeds 30` and reports raw
runs, mean, standard deviation, median, and a 95% normal interval.

## Constitutional emergency control

`god-owner-control` shares the product's canonical data/organism paths. Its phrase
is a temporary local fallback authentication factor pending a future passkey,
Windows Hello, or hardware-key design. The real phrase must never be stored,
logged, documented, passed as an argument, or exposed to cognition. Signed state
is refreshed at governed boundaries and reconciled monotonically toward greater
restriction; malformed state fails closed. STOP does not depend on a model or a
Saphira and survives restart.
