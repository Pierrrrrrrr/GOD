# Digital ontogeny migration target

Phase 2B.5 does **not** implement the Morphogenesis Lab and does not rename or
rewrite the Phase 1 `DigitalNeuron` runtime. Backward compatibility is required.
This document is the target for a later, evidence-driven migration.

The fundamental evolvable unit will be a pluripotent **DigitalCell**. A neuron is
one phenotype, not the only possible cell. A future Digital Meristem will preserve
pluripotent cells while transient organoids may form and dissolve.

Saphira-A and Saphira-B have permanent, separate developmental lineages. Each
sister develops private cognition, memory, and morphology. Neither sister may
overwrite, absorb, or evolve into the other lineage. Together, A+B and their shared
external G.O.D. Body form the higher-order twin superorganism called G.O.D.

The sisters may use the shared Body only through an authority-controlled `BodyBus`.
The shared Root Constitution remains outside evolution. Root authority, including
owner approval and credential custody, is never a cellular phenotype.

Future experiments may investigate morphogen fields, stigmergy, lateral inhibition,
tropisms, resource source-sink dynamics, canalization, immune waves, and
regeneration. These are hypotheses: developmental laws will be empirically tested
in M0 rather than hardcoded as truth.
